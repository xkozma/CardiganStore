Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

QR Wallet is designed to keep your loyalty cards and personal information on your device. This app is built with a local-first approach and does not require creating an account or sending your data to a remote server.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, address, or payment details. The app stores card information locally on your phone for functionality.

  • Card names and stored QR/barcode values
  • Images or screenshots you import into the app
  • Local app preferences needed for wallet usage

This information is kept on the device and is not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly choose to share or export a wallet file outside the app yourself.

2. Camera and photo access

The app may request camera access to scan QR codes or barcodes directly. It may also request access to images/photos to import a saved QR code or card image from your device.

These permissions are used only for scanning and importing your card data. We do not use them for tracking, advertising, or uploading your photos to a third-party service.

3. Local-only storage

QR Wallet stores card data directly on the device. It is not designed to sync your wallet to a cloud service or online account. Your wallet stays local to your phone unless you choose to export or share it.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties. The only time your data may leave the device is if you manually choose to:

  • export a wallet file
  • share a QR code or card image via another app
  • send a shared wallet payload to another person

Any data shared in those cases is under your control and the responsibility of the receiving app or person.

5. Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, ad networks, or remote tracking SDKs. We do not currently transmit your wallet data to a server or third-party service.

6. Security

Because the app stores information locally on your device, your security depends on the security of your phone. We recommend locking your device, using biometric protection, and keeping the app updated.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete individual cards from the app at any time. If you uninstall the app, locally stored card data may be removed with the app, depending on your device and app data settings. If you want to keep a backup, export your wallet before uninstalling.

8. Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will revise the date above. Continued use of the app after such changes means you accept the updated policy.
